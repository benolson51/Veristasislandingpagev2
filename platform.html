SmartScope™

From 110 controls to ~30–40 real decisions.

A full 110-control interrogation is where most projects stall. SmartScope prunes scope and applies stack inheritance — if you're on M365 GCC High, Google Workspace, or AWS GovCloud, the controls those stacks satisfy collapse out of your queue. Every "not applicable" is logged with an assessor-credible rationale, so the reduction holds up under review.

  • Scope-pruning down to the decisions that actually apply
  • Stack inheritance for common GovCloud / GCC High setups
  • Every N/A logged with a documented rationale
smartscope.sample.yml 
# sample intake reduction
controls_total: 110
inherited_stack: m365-gcc-high
pruned_not_applicable: 71
decisions_remaining: 39
na_rationale: logged-per-control
scorepath.sample.yml 
# sample roadmap — illustrative
current_sprs: 62
target_sprs: 88
score_range: -203 .. 110
must_meet_gating: 1
quick_wins: 9
ranked_by: points-per-effort
SPRS score + ScorePath™

Your real score, with a path to raise it.

VeriStasis computes the DoD Assessment Methodology score (110 maximum, down to a −203 floor) from your control status. ScorePath then ranks the remaining work by points-per-effort, separating the must-meet gating controls from the quick-wins, and projects "from SPRS X to Y, do these N things." A number alone is a false promise; a number with a legitimate roadmap is a plan.

  • Real DoD Assessment Methodology score
  • Gating controls separated from quick-wins
  • "SPRS X → Y, do these N things" projection
What gets generated

The artifacts an assessment needs.

Generated from your answers against a real NIST SP 800-171 Rev 2 control table — narratives, point values, gating flags, and POA&M scaffolds all built in.

System Security Plan

The full SSP — 14-family narratives, system description, boundary, CUI data flows, and asset categorization — generated as Microsoft Word and OSCAL JSON, so it's readable by humans and machine-consumable by tooling alike.

ssp_export.manifest 
ssp_docx: generated
ssp_oscal_json: generated
families: 14
boundary: described
cui_data_flows: documented
checksum: sha-256

POA&M

One remediation record per unmet or partial control: the weakness, the remediation, an owner, milestones, a 180-day closeout, and lifecycle status.

Policy set

Roughly 20 documents — one policy per NIST family plus key procedures — each linked to the controls it satisfies.

Export bundle

One zip an RPO or C3PAO consumes directly: SSP (docx + OSCAL), POA&M, policies, SPRS worksheet (PDF), and an evidence index — with per-file and whole-bundle SHA-256 checksums.

C3PAO package

A per-control assessor index — status, the documents that cover it, evidence count, POA&M status — and a plain-language ASSESSOR_README with checksum-verification instructions.

CRM + ESP service description

An auto-generated, per-practice Customer Responsibility Matrix and an ESP service description — the artifact your assessor needs when listing VeriStasis as an External Service Provider in your SSP.

LiveDoc™ & maintenance

A controlled corpus that stays current.

Your documentation is a versioned, control-linked, checksummed system of record — not a one-time deliverable. Show any control on demand, track stale policies, recompute after a change, and walk into the annual affirmation with blockers surfaced ahead of time. When a C3PAO finding lands, the rejection→remediation loop flips the control to Not Met, tracks it, and lets you resolve and re-attest.

  • One-click "show me this control" + stale-policy tracking
  • Recompute, 180-day closeout, annual affirmation-readiness
