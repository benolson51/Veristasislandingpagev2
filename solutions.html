For defense contractors

You have a CUI obligation and a deadline.

A prime flow-down or a solicitation with a CMMC clause just made Level 2 your problem — and you have no internal GRC or CISO function. VeriStasis turns your attested answers into the complete artifact set, computes your real SPRS score, and shows the fastest legitimate path to a passing number, so the owner who signs the affirmation knows exactly where things stand.

  • The three mandatory artifacts — SSP, POA&M, policy set
  • A real SPRS score with a ScorePath™ roadmap
  • A C3PAO-ready, checksummed export bundle
  • Maintenance through the annual affirmation

Typical fit

A DIB contractor of roughly 20–200 people handling CUI, needing CMMC Level 2 (110 practices), with no in-house compliance team — and an owner, CEO, or senior official who personally signs the SPRS affirmation.

Built for the channel

Each client organization is separated at the application level — an org only ever sees its own assessments. Generate the ESP service description and Customer Responsibility Matrix your clients' assessors ask for, and stand behind deterministic output in front of a C3PAO.

Resell VeriStasis across your contractor clients as margin expansion, and replace hand-built SSPs with a repeatable, controlled corpus. Because generation is deterministic, the documents you deliver are consistent and explainable — which matters when your reputation is on every engagement.
