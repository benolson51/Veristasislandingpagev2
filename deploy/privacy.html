Privacy Policy.

How we collect, use, and protect your information — and how your uploaded evidence stays encrypted in your browser.

Last updated: June 16, 2026

1. Who we are

VeriStasis is a DBA of Ensight LLC, based in Kingsburg, California ("VeriStasis," "we," "us"). This Privacy Policy describes how we handle information in connection with our website and the VeriStasis documentation platform (together, the "Service"). VeriStasis is a software-assisted documentation and document-control service; it is not a hosting environment for your live systems or your Controlled Unclassified Information.

2. Information we collect

Information you provide. When you request a demo or contact us, we collect your name, work email, company name, your role, and where you are in your CMMC journey. When you create an account, we collect your email address and a password credential that we store only in protected (hashed) form.

Customer Content. "Customer Content" includes the answers you attest to, the control-status data and SPRS scores derived from them, the documents we generate for you, and any evidence files you choose to upload. Evidence files are encrypted in your browser with AES-256-GCM before they reach us, and our servers store only ciphertext we cannot decrypt; these files are recoverable with your vault passphrase, which we never receive. Your control-status data, scores, and generated documents are stored on our servers and are readable by the platform so that we can produce and maintain them for you.

Security and usage data. We automatically collect authentication events, IP addresses, timestamps, and related security telemetry to operate and protect your account.

3. How we use information

  • To provide, operate, and secure the Service
  • To generate the documents, control mappings, SPRS scores, and roadmaps you ask the platform to produce
  • To respond to demo requests and support inquiries
  • To meet our own legal and contractual obligations

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use Customer Content for advertising or for training machine-learning models — generation is deterministic and template-based.

4. Customer Content and CUI

Your content is yours. You retain all rights to your Customer Content. We do not connect to or ingest data from your live systems, and we are not a CUI hosting environment. Uploaded evidence is encrypted in your browser before it reaches us; we hold no recovery key for it.

To be precise: your control-status answers, computed scores, and the documents we generate are stored server-side and are readable by the platform in order to deliver the Service. The "we cannot read it" protection applies specifically to your uploaded evidence files. You remain responsible for the lawful handling, marking, and authorization of any Controlled Unclassified Information (CUI) and for your obligations under DFARS 252.204-7012, NIST SP 800-171, and any export-control requirements that apply to your organization.

5. How we share information

We share information only with: (a) infrastructure and service providers who host and support the Service under confidentiality obligations; (b) authorities when required by law, subpoena, or to protect rights and safety; and (c) a successor entity in the event of a merger or acquisition, subject to this policy. We never sell or rent personal information to third parties.

6. Security

We protect uploaded evidence with client-side AES-256-GCM encryption (encrypted in your browser; we store ciphertext only). Generated artifacts are SHA-256 checksummed and version-controlled, and each organization's data is separated at the application level so an organization sees only its own assessments. Account access uses email and password today; additional multi-factor options are on our roadmap. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we design for data minimization — holding as little of your data as possible.

7. Data retention

We retain account information for as long as your account is active and as needed to meet legal obligations. Customer Content is retained per your subscription agreement; upon termination, you may export your documentation and evidence, after which it is deleted from active systems on a defined schedule.

8. Your rights and choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by contacting us through our contact page. California residents may have additional rights under the CCPA/CPRA, including the right to know and the right to delete. We do not discriminate against anyone for exercising privacy rights.

9. Cookies and third parties

Our website does not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies. Pages load fonts and icon assets from third-party CDNs (Google Fonts, unpkg), which may receive standard request data such as your IP address when those assets load.

10. Children

The Service is a business product for the defense supply chain and is not directed to anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from minors.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date, and account holders will be notified of significant changes.

12. Contact us

Questions about this policy or your data? Reach us through the contact page or write to: Ensight LLC (DBA VeriStasis), Kingsburg, California.
