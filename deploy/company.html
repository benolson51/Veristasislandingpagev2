Our story

A father and son, building the honest CMMC tool.

VeriStasis is a two-person company — Brian Olson, a CMMC Registered Practitioner who has spent his career in cybersecurity and compliance, and his son Benjamin. We built it for the small defense contractors everyone else overcharges, overpromises, or overlooks.

Why we built it

The shops that keep the country running can't afford the help they're told to buy.

A 20-to-200-person machine shop gets a CMMC clause flowed down from a prime, with a deadline — and suddenly owes a System Security Plan, a POA&M, a policy set, and an SPRS score, with the owner's name personally on the affirmation. The options on the table were a ~$40k, multi-month consultant engagement, or generic GRC software too abstract to produce the actual documents.

We watched good companies get stuck in that gap. So we built the thing in the middle: software-fast and fixed-price, with a real CMMC expert checking the work before anyone signs.

What we refused to build
An AI black box you can't explain to an assessor
A one-time PDF that goes stale in SharePoint
A tool that overpromises what it can do
A $40k retainer with an open meter
The two of us

Decades of expertise, and the drive to put it in your hands.

BO
Brian Olson
Co-Founder · CMMC RP · the expert who reviews your work

Brian is a CMMC Registered Practitioner (RP) with 30 years in cybersecurity, compliance, and GRC — credentials including CISSP, CISA, CISM, CDPSE, CCSP, HCISPP, and ISO 27001/42001 Lead Implementer — across startups, healthcare, manufacturing, and government. He's worked CMMC since the program's early days. Every VeriStasis package gets his review before a customer certifies. "For 30 years I've been the one responsible for security and compliance — building the programs and sitting for the audits — so I review your package the way the official who signs your affirmation has to."

BO
Benjamin Olson
Co-Founder · sales & customer relationships

Ben's background is in finance — which taught him to read a business by its numbers and its risks. But the part he loves is the part a spreadsheet never captures: the people. He's driven by sales done the right way — listening hard, building real relationships, and solving the problem in front of him instead of pushing a product. VeriStasis is where that meets a mission he believes in: helping the small defense contractors who keep our country safe become CMMC-compliant without being buried in paperwork or bled dry by consultants. Building it alongside his dad — pairing Brian's 30 years of cybersecurity expertise with his own obsession for the customer — is the work he's proudest of.

What we believe

Our name is on it, like yours is on the affirmation.

When the buyer carries personal liability for what they sign, the most valuable thing we can do is be exact — generate what's real, never claim what isn't, and make the documentation hold up when an assessor reads it.

Honesty as a feature

We say exactly what the product does — and what it doesn't. For a buyer who's been burned by vaporware, that restraint is the point.

Software, with a human check

The engine does the heavy lifting; a credentialed CMMC RP reviews it before you certify. Speed and judgment, not one at the expense of the other.

Built for the small shop

We're a small team too. We build for the contractors the big platforms serve generically and the big consultants price out.
