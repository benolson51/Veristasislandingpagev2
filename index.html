Why now

The clock is real — and it's personal.

CMMC Level 2 is phasing into DoD contracts now. The contractors who get ahead of it keep bidding; the ones who wait get caught flat.

The phase-in is underway

Starting November 10, 2026, DoD can begin requiring a Level 2 C3PAO certificate on new contracts — and a prime can ask for your SPRS score today. If a flow-down letter has landed, your clock already started.

Readiness takes months

Authorized assessor capacity is scarce relative to the tens of thousands of contractors in scope, and getting documentation assessment-ready isn't a weekend job. First-come fills the calendar.

You sign it personally

A senior official signs the SPRS affirmation, and that signature carries personal False Claims Act exposure. A wrong number isn't just a lost bid — it's your name on it.

What it does

Your answers in.
An assessment-ready evidence set out.

VeriStasis is a software-assisted documentation and document-control service — you attest, it generates, controls, and keeps everything current. It does not connect to or monitor your live systems.

ScorePath™ — the fastest legitimate path to a passing score

Most tools hand you a number. ScorePath ranks the work by points-per-effort, separates the must-meet gating controls from the quick-wins, and projects "from SPRS X to Y, do these N things." Your real DoD Assessment Methodology score, with a roadmap — never a false promise.

SmartScope™

Adaptive intake collapses the 110-control interrogation to roughly 30–40 real decisions using scope-pruning and stack inheritance (M365 GCC High, Google Workspace, AWS GovCloud). Every N/A is logged with an assessor-credible rationale.

LiveDoc™

A living, version-controlled, control-linked system of record — not a one-time deliverable that goes stale in SharePoint. One click shows you any control, with stale-policy tracking built in.

The three mandatory artifacts

A complete System Security Plan (Word and OSCAL JSON), a POA&M with one remediation record per unmet control, and a ~20-document policy set — one policy per NIST family, control-linked.

C3PAO-ready bundle

One zip an RPO or C3PAO consumes directly: SSP, POA&M, policies, SPRS worksheet, a per-control assessor index, and a plain-language README — with per-file and whole-bundle SHA-256 checksums.

Client-side encrypted evidence

Evidence is encrypted in your browser with AES-256-GCM before it reaches us — the server stores only ciphertext it cannot decrypt. Recoverable with your vault passphrase; we hold no recovery key.

How we handle your data

Honest about what we hold.

We say exactly what the product does. Data minimization is the design, not a tagline.

Encrypted in your browser

Your evidence is encrypted in your browser (AES-256-GCM) before it reaches us — the server stores only ciphertext it cannot decrypt. Recoverable with your vault passphrase; we hold no recovery key.

We don't touch your live systems

We generate your documentation from the answers you provide — we do not connect to or ingest data from your live systems, and we are not a CUI hosting environment.

Data minimization by design

We hold as little of your data as possible. The intake is scoped to the decisions an assessor actually needs to see — nothing more.

Checksummed & version-controlled

Every artifact is SHA-256 checksummed and version-controlled, so you — and your assessor — can verify exactly what was produced and when.

Plainly: your control-status data, scores, and the generated documents are stored on our servers and are readable by the platform so we can produce and maintain them. The "we can't read it" guarantee applies specifically to your uploaded evidence files. We don't claim FedRAMP authorization or equivalency; the platform is designed for a Security Protection Data (SPD-only) posture, with classification pending review by qualified counsel.
