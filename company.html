About us

Compliance documentation, without the hype.

VeriStasis is a DBA of Ensight LLC, based in Kingsburg, California. We build a deterministic documentation engine that helps small defense contractors get to CMMC Level 2 — and we say exactly what it does, and what it doesn't.

Leadership

VeriStasis was co-founded by Brian Olson and Benjamin Olson. We built it because the contractors who most need CMMC Level 2 help are the ones least able to staff a GRC team for it — and the tooling they were handed too often overpromised.
