Security & data handling

Honest about what we hold.

We'd rather tell you exactly what the product does than dress it up. Here is how VeriStasis handles your data — including the parts the platform can read, stated plainly.

Talk it through with us

Evidence encrypted in your browser

The sensitive proof you upload to the vault is encrypted in your browser with AES-256-GCM before it reaches us. The server stores only ciphertext it cannot decrypt. It's recoverable with your vault passphrase — and we hold no recovery key, so if you lose the passphrase, we cannot restore the file for you. That's the trade-off of a zero-access design, and we make it on purpose.

evidence_vault.facts 
client_side_encryption: AES-256-GCM
encrypted_before_upload: true
server_stores: ciphertext-only
recovery_key_held_by_us: none
recoverable_with: your-passphrase

No connection to your systems

We generate your documentation from the answers you provide. We do not connect to or ingest data from your live systems, and we are not a CUI hosting environment, a SIEM, or a monitoring platform.

Data minimization by design

We hold as little of your data as possible. SmartScope™ narrows intake to the decisions an assessment actually turns on, so we're not collecting more than the work requires.

No fine print

What we can & can't read.

A "we never see your data" line would be false, so we won't write it. Here is the real boundary.

We can't read

  • Your uploaded evidence files — encrypted in your browser, stored as ciphertext only
  • Anything protected by your vault passphrase, which we never receive
VS

We can read (to run the service)

  • Your control-status answers and computed SPRS scores
  • The documents we generate for you — SSP, POA&M, policies
  • Account and security metadata needed to operate your tenant
Integrity

Checksummed & version-controlled.

Every artifact is SHA-256 checksummed and version-controlled. The export bundle ships per-file and whole-bundle checksums plus a README with verification instructions, so you — and your assessor — can confirm exactly what was produced and that it hasn't changed since.

  • Per-file and whole-bundle SHA-256 checksums
  • Versioned, control-linked system of record
  • Application-level separation between organizations
Regulatory posture
