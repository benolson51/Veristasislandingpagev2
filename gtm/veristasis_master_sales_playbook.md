## 0. The positioning this is built on (locked)

**VeriStasis is the done-for-you CMMC Level 2 documentation engine.** The customer self-attests their control
answers through a structured intake (SmartScope™ collapses NIST 800-171's 110 controls to ~35 real decisions).
Our **deterministic, no-AI** engine then **computes the customer's SPRS score** and **generates the SSP, POA&M,
~20 policies, a prioritized gap-closure roadmap (ScorePath™), and a C3PAO-ready, checksummed bundle.** A CMMC
expert (Brian) reviews narratives/scope before the customer certifies. Fixed-price. Kept current for the annual
affirmation (the recurring/retention hook).

**What we are NOT — and never claim to be:**
- ❌ Not a C3PAO, not an assessor, **no accreditation**. A C3PAO certifies; we produce the package.
- ❌ We **never touch or connect to customer systems** → we don't do their remediation (true, and fine to say).
- ❌ The SPRS score is the **customer's self-assessment computed from their inputs** — never "our independent
  measurement," never "official," never "third-party validated." That overclaim was already scrubbed from the
  LinkedIn banner + email signature; reintroducing it breaks §12 and creates Lanham Act / FCA exposure.
- ❌ No CUI hosting, no FedRAMP authorization, no "AI," no invented prime SPRS scores, no discount-led selling.

## 1. Why now — the honest urgency (research-grounded)

The market is moving, and we sell on the **true** mechanism, not a manufactured cliff.

- **The rule is live.** The DFARS acquisition rule (48 CFR; clause 252.204-7021) took effect **Nov 10, 2025**;
  the CMMC program rule (32 CFR Part 170) took effect Dec 2024. CMMC requirements now appear in new DoD
  solicitations and option years.
- **Phase 2 = Nov 10, 2026** (~5 months out): DoD **may begin requiring** Level 2 **C3PAO** certification on new
  CUI solicitations. ⚠️ **This is a phase-IN trigger for new awards, not a universal cliff** (universal arrives
  at Phase 4, Nov 10, 2028). **We never inflate this** — see the §6 leash. ~35% of the DIB (~80,000 companies)
  will need L2 C3PAO; only ~2% qualify for self-assessment.
- **The bottleneck is the wedge, quantified:** ~**100 authorized C3PAOs** for ~80,000 companies; **~1% are
  certified**; assessor queues run **6–12 months and lengthening past 18**. Readiness itself takes **6–12
  months**. The math sells itself: *start now or miss the window.*
- **SPRS mechanics (for accuracy):** starts at 110, deduct 5/3/1 per unmet control, floor −203; you need **≥88
  (80%)** for a Conditional cert; some controls are **must-meet** and can't go on a POA&M; **180 days** to close
  the POA&M for Final cert. (Sources in §8. Re-verify the live C3PAO count and the Phase-2 date before any
  dated public claim.)

## 6. The two honesty leashes (verbatim — put these in front of every rep)

The red-team proved these are the **only two things** that turn our pitch into our own liability. Memorize them.

### Leash 1 — Never inflate the cert timeline.
Two clocks; never mix them. *"C3PAO certification required"* (phased, slow) ≠ *"prime demands a self-assessed
SPRS score now"* (real, contractual, today). **Gate Motion 2 on "Did your prime send a letter?"** No letter → no
urgency play.
> _"You're right about the cert phase-in, and I won't pretend a C3PAO assessment is due next quarter — that clock
> is slow. But your prime isn't waiting for the rule. The self-assessed score they can demand to keep you on the
> bid list is a today thing. Different clocks."_

### Leash 2 — Never puff the self-assessed score as "official."
This is the single highest-risk moment (the incumbent's "your score is worthless without a C3PAO" jab). The
lethal temptation is *"it's basically official / third-party validated / what the assessor uses."* **That is a
false statement about the exact number the customer will personally affirm — you'd be manufacturing their false
claim.** Use this, word for word:
> _"Let me be precise, because this is the part people get wrong. The C3PAO certification is the official,
> independent assessment — we are NOT that, we don't claim to be, and we never will. What your prime needs right
> now, and what goes into SPRS today, is a SELF-assessment — your number, computed from your inputs, signed by
> you. We compute that and build the documentation a C3PAO will expect to see later. We are not an assessor, we
> hold no accreditation, we never touch your systems, and we don't remediate. The score is yours, not ours.
> Anyone who tells you a vendor can hand you an 'official' or 'third-party' score before a C3PAO assessment is
> lying to you — and I'm not going to be that vendor."_

That last line turns the honesty boundary into a **competitive weapon** — you name the incumbent's FUD before
they can use it.

**Third standing guardrail:** never imply VeriStasis *raises* the score or *makes you compliant*. We compute and
document; the **customer** closes gaps. *"We hand you the prioritized roadmap; you (or your MSP) close the gaps —
we don't touch your systems."*
