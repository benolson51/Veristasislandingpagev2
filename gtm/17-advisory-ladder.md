# 17 — Products & Services: the Advisory Ladder

_How consulting layers onto the engine — monetizing the human / CMMC-RP angle (Brian) without breaking §12,
the channel, or your capacity. **Prices below are PUBLIC on `solutions.html` (set 2026-06-18, prior draft − 10%);**

## The one rule that decides everything: advisory, NOT implementation
- ✅ **Advisory** = advise, interpret controls, define scope, review the package, prioritize the fix list,
  coordinate with the assessor. **No systems touched.**
- ❌ **Implementation / remediation** = configure M365, deploy MFA, manage their IT. **Never.**

Crossing that line breaks three things at once: the **§12 honesty ruling** ("we don't touch your systems /
don't do remediation"), the **MSP channel** (you'd compete with the partners you want referring you), and
your **capacity** (implementation is an unbounded hourly sink — the $40k/2-month slog you're positioned
*against*). Stay advisory and all three stay intact.

**Scope at a glance — IN vs OUT (the anti-creep firewall):**
| ✅ IN (fixed-price advisory) | ❌ OUT (creep / "turning wrenches") |
|---|---|
| Review SSP/POA&M/policies for assessor-credibility (narratives, scope, N/A) | Configuring/implementing controls (MFA, M365, FIPS crypto) |
| Sanity-check the SPRS score vs. the client's *attested* status | Network/system architecture; running their environment (SIEM/MDR) |
| Flag must-meet gating gaps + POA&M-eligibility | Representing them to the C3PAO / "certifying" them |
| Contextualize risk + prioritize the gap roadmap (ScorePath) | Legal opinions (→ counsel); unlimited hourly hand-holding (→ retainer/RP Partner) |

**RP guardrail:** advise/prepare ≠ assess. Always: *"As a CMMC Registered Practitioner we prepare and advise;
a C3PAO assesses."* Even a "readiness review" is **your package/readiness, reviewed** — never an official
pre-assessment or a graded determination.

## Packaging model
Keep the engine tiers (**Audit Foundation / C3PAO-Ready Package / Always Current**) as the scalable core. Add consulting two ways:
1. **À la carte advisory add-ons** — fixed-scope, fixed-price, scheduled.
2. **A premium tier — "RP Partner"** = Always Current + direct RP advisory throughout.

> **Two distinct "human" things — keep them separate so nothing looks double-sold:**
> (a) **Included expert review** (in every tier) = the RP checks the *documents we generated* before you certify.
> (b) **Advisory** (add-ons + RP Partner) = *proactive* RP time that goes beyond reviewing our docs.

## The discipline that keeps it from sinking you
Every advisory offer must be: **fixed-scope · fixed-price · scheduled against Brian's calendar ·
concurrency-capped (his bandwidth) · waitlisted when full · always routing toward Always Current/RP Partner recurring.**
The moment it becomes open-ended hourly work, you've rebuilt the consultant slog. Productize ruthlessly.

## Channel: advisory makes the division of labor clean (and conflict-free)
- **MSPs:** "You do the hands-on implementation; we do the docs + advisory." No competition for their
  revenue → better partners and referrers.
- **C3PAOs:** you're now a fuller prep partner they can refer the *whole* readiness to (they're barred from
  prepping clients they assess). Lead the channel with C3PAOs.
