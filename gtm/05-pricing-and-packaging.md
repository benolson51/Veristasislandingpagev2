# 05 — Pricing & Packaging

> **DRAFT — internal only.** Dollar figures here are working numbers from the breakdown's §10 and are
> **not validated**. Do **not** publish them on the site or in cold copy. Anchor public messaging against the
> **consultant baseline** (a market fact) and quote real numbers only on a call or written quote.

## Packages (lead with scope, not price)
| Tier | What it includes | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| **Starter** | SSP + POA&M + SPRS worksheet + ScorePath™ roadmap | Shops that need a credible score and a plan, fast |
| **Complete** | Everything in Starter + the full ~20-doc policy set + the C3PAO-ready bundle (assessor index + README + checksums) + CRM/ESP | Shops heading into an actual assessment |
| **Managed** | Everything in Complete + LiveDoc™ control, quarterly review, affirmation prep, unlimited re-docs on change | Staying affirmation-ready year over year |

The **Managed** tier is the business: it's the retention hook (the annual affirmation recurs; a one-time SSP
churns ~25–40%/yr without it). Always present Managed as the default "and then we keep it current."

## Draft economics (INTERNAL — validate before using)
| Tier | Setup (one-time) | Recurring | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Starter | ~$8k | ~$300/mo | Entry; upgrade path to Complete |
| Complete | ~$12k | ~$600/mo | Most common land for assessment-bound shops |
| Managed | ~$15k | ~$1,000/mo | Retention tier; lead here on renewal |

- **Anchor 1 (the closer):** a consultant SSP engagement is commonly **~$40k and 4–8 weeks**. VeriStasis is a
  **fraction of that, fixed-price, in weeks** — with an expert review before you certify.
- **Anchor 2 (recurring sanity):** incumbent SMPL-C recurring is roughly **$4k/yr (L1) / $6k/yr (L2)** per
  org. Our recurring should be defensible next to that; lead on the deliverable depth + the bundle + the
  workflow they under-serve, **not** a price war to the bottom.
- **Setup funds the business; recurring is the business.** Use setup fees for cash flow; optimize for getting
  shops onto a recurring (ideally Managed) plan.

## Early-customer / pilot discounting (draft, founder discretion)
- Discount or waive **setup** for the first 3–5 design partners in exchange for candid feedback + (only if
  earned) a reference. Keep **recurring near standard** so you're validating real willingness to pay, not
  buying logos.
- Cap discounted pilots at review capacity (~3–5 concurrent). Beyond that, waitlist at standard pricing.

## "How we quote" rubric (keeps quoting fast and capacity-aware)
1. Confirm **scope** from intake: number of systems/boundaries, stack (inheritance reduces work), how much
   existing documentation can be imported.
2. Confirm **timeline** vs. the deadline. Tight timelines = Complete/Managed, not Starter.
3. Confirm **who signs** and that they're bought in.
4. Map to a tier; quote setup + recurring as **one fixed number**, no hourly meter.
5. **Check review capacity** before promising a turnaround — never sell a date Brian can't review by.
6. Default the proposal to **Complete or Managed**; offer Starter only as a down-sell to keep a deal alive.

## What NOT to do
- Don't publish these dollar figures publicly or put them in cold email.
- Don't quote a number before you've confirmed scope + who signs.
- Don't promise a turnaround you can't expert-review in time.
- Don't claim certification, FedRAMP, CUI hosting, or anything in §12's "never" list to justify price.
