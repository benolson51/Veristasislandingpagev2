## Why the finishing/casting/harness verticals matter (pitch language)

A machine shop can argue about whether its parts are CUI. A **NADCAP finisher or foundry can't** — the
**drawing itself** is the controlled item, and they receive dozens a week from primes. Same 110 controls,
same SPRS requirement, almost zero CMMC vendors talking to them. Expect less pitch-fatigue than machine shops.
