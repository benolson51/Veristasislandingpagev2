# 12 — First Paid Deal in 14 Days (warm-led)

_The single objective: **one signed, full-paid customer by Day 14.** Warm-first, Ben-led, Brian on
technical/onboarding. Window: **Tue Jun 16 → Mon Jun 29, 2026.** All copy is §12-clean._

> Why warm-first wins the 14-day race: a referral/known contact compresses trust (the hard part for a buyer
> signing an affirmation) AND it skips the ~2-week cold-email warmup — warm outreach goes from your real
> inbox, phone, and LinkedIn today. Cold is a background fill, not the driver.

## Before Day 1: clear the Go/No-Go gate
You can't close in 14 days if there's nothing to sign or no way to pay. Confirm (details in
[13-close-kit.md](13-close-kit.md) and the QA report):

- [ ] **[Brian]** demo environment ready to screen-share
- [ ] Fixed-price **quote/proposal** template ready
- [ ] Simple **order form / letter of agreement** ready
- [ ] **Payment/invoicing** set up (Stripe invoice or ACH) — *human task, do before outreach*
- [ ] Calendar link live; LinkedIn/site messaging consistent (already done)
If a box is blocked, who unblocks it and by when goes in the QA report.

## Step 1 — Build & rank the warm list (Day 1)
Inventory fast from where your relationships already live:
- Phone contacts, email, and **LinkedIn 1st-degree** — anyone in/around the DIB.
- **2nd-degree intros:** mutual connections who can introduce you to a contractor's owner/CEO.
- Past colleagues/customers, local **manufacturing/defense** community, **APEX Accelerator** contacts,
  industry groups, your own prior conversations where CMMC ever came up.

**Rank each by closeability (score 0–2 on each, sort by total):**
| Signal | 2 | 1 | 0 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Handles **CUI** (real L2) | confirmed | likely | FCI-only/unknown |
| **Deadline/trigger** | prime flow-down w/ date | bidding/asking for SSP | none |
| **Signer reachable** | you know the signer | warm intro available | cold |
| **Warmth** | can meet this week | knows you | weak tie |

Target a **shortlist of 5–15**. Pick **"The One"** — the single highest-scoring account you can realistically
sign by Day 14 — and give it disproportionate attention while the rest progress.

## Fast-close objection handling (compress the decision)
- **"Let me think about it."** → "Totally fair. The thing that's hard to get back is time — Phase 2 starts
  Nov 10 and assessor slots fill. If we start your intake this week you're ahead of that. What's the one thing
  you'd want answered to feel good moving now?"
- **"Send me info."** → "Will do — but the fastest way to judge it is the 20-minute screen-share where you
  see your own scope. Grab a time? [calendar link]"
- **"Price?"** → quote the fixed number for the tier; anchor: "a consultant SSP is commonly ~$40k and two
  months; this is fixed-price and weeks, expert-reviewed."
- **"Is it just software?"** → "Software does the heavy lifting; a CMMC expert reviews your narratives, scope,
  and score before you certify. You get the speed and the human check."

## Founding-customer framing — value, not discount (it's a full paid deal)
Compress the "yes" without cutting price:
- **Founding-customer priority onboarding** (front of the queue before Phase-2 capacity tightens).
- **Locked-in pricing** for the term.
- **Direct founder access** (you + Brian) through their first affirmation.
- Honest urgency: the Phase-2 deadline + scarce assessor capacity are real, not manufactured.

## Risk + fallback
- **No verbal yes by Day 9 (Jun 24):** widen — send 5 more intro asks, work the next tier of the shortlist,
  and ask every warm contact "who else should I be talking to?" Referrals beget referrals.
- **Too much demand vs Brian's review capacity:** book the deal now, schedule onboarding to a date Brian
  confirms — never promise a turnaround he can't hit. Protecting delivery protects the affirmation.
- **Deal stalls on paperwork/payment:** that's a gate failure — fix [13] before more outreach.
