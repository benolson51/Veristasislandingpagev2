## 30-day targets (the scoreboard)

|---|---|---|

Right-sized for **one seller (Ben)**; the warm 14-day push for the *first* paid deal is tracked separately in
[12-first-paid-deal-14-day-sprint.md](12-first-paid-deal-14-day-sprint.md). Capacity reminder: onboarding is
capped by **Brian's review bandwidth (~3–5 intakes/week)** — demos scale, paid delivery does not; waitlist
rather than overpromise turnaround.

## Leading vs lagging indicators
- **Leading (control these daily):** new prospects researched, touches sent, LinkedIn connects, calls dialed,
  follow-ups completed. If these hit, the funnel fills.
- **Lagging (review weekly):** reply %, demos booked, pilots, paid, waitlist. Use them to recalibrate the
  leading targets and the message.

## Daily minimums during live outreach (Weeks 2–4)
- ~6–10 new prospects contacted/day (solo), ~all follow-ups due cleared, every reply answered same day,
  every booked demo confirmed. Log it (see activity tracker).

## Weekly review ritual (Friday, 30 min — Ben; loop Brian on delivery)
1. Pull the numbers (targets vs actual, both leading and lagging).
2. Reply-rate check: if <5%, fix targeting or subject lines before adding volume.
3. Demo→pilot check: if demos aren't converting, fix the demo or qualification, not the top of funnel.
4. Pick the ONE bottleneck for next week and assign an owner.
5. Re-forecast the month from real rates.

## Definition of "working" by end of 30 days
- Reply rate ≥5% and ≥8 demos → **message + targeting validated**.
- ≥1 paid deal or ≥3 active pilots → **offer + price validated**.
- ≥2 RPO calls held → **channel hypothesis advancing** (the real scale lever).
If reply rate is strong but demos don't convert → fix the demo/offer. If replies are weak → fix targeting or
copy. Decide month-2 focus (more direct vs. lean into channel) from these signals.
