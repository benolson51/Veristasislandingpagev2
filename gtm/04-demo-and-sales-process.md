# 04 — Demo & Sales Process

_A demo that shows the wedge, an honest expert-review step, and a pipeline you can run from a spreadsheet._

## The demo (30 minutes, screen-share)
Goal: get from "interesting" to "run my intake." Show, don't tell.

1. **Frame (2 min).** "You answer a scoped intake; it generates your mandatory documents and your real SPRS
   score; an expert reviews before you certify; you walk into the assessment with a checksummed package.
   No AI — it's deterministic and explainable." Confirm their trigger/timeline from discovery.
2. **SmartScope™ intake (6 min).** Run a few real questions live. Show one answer pruning many controls to
   N/A **with the auto-written justification**, and stack inheritance (e.g. M365 GCC High) auto-answering
   inherited controls "Met." Land the line: "110 controls just became ~30–40 real decisions."
3. **Generation (6 min).** Open a generated **SSP** (Word + show the OSCAL JSON exists), the **POA&M** with a
   gating control flagged "must-meet — not POA&M-eligible," and the **policy set**. Emphasize deterministic:
   same inputs → same output.
4. **SPRS score + ScorePath™ (6 min).** Show the score, then the two ranked lists — **gating** vs.
   **quick-wins by points-per-effort** — and the "from X → Y, do these N things" headline. This is the
   a-ha: "most tools give you a number; this gives you the to-do list."
5. **The bundle + close (5 min).** Export `assessment_bundle.zip`; open the MANIFEST; show the
   **re-verifiable whole-bundle SHA-256** and the assessor index + README. _"Here's your assessment package,
   and here's the checksum your C3PAO verifies."_
6. **Honesty beat (2 min).** State plainly what it is **not** (not a SIEM, doesn't host CUI, doesn't touch
   live systems, not FedRAMP, not the assessor) and that a CMMC expert reviews before certification. For a
   signer under FCA exposure, this is what earns trust.
7. **Next step (3 min).** Book the intake + name the price (see pricing). Don't end on "I'll send info."

## Qualification framework (MEDDIC-lite, run during discovery + demo)
- **Metric/why-now:** the deadline (flow-down date / bid / Phase-2). No date = nurture, not now.
- **Economic buyer:** the affirmation-signer is engaged. If you're only talking to IT, get the signer in.
- **Decision criteria:** assessor-credible docs, fixed price, speed, an expert check.
- **Pain:** consultant cost/time, low/no SPRS score, prime pressure.
- **Champion:** the person who feels the deadline.
- **Disqualifiers:** FCI/L1 only, no CUI, internal GRC org.

## Pilot / design-partner offer (early-customer engine)
- **Who:** 3–5 strong-fit contractors who'll actually run the intake and give feedback.
- **Scope:** full pipeline (intake → generated artifacts → SPRS + ScorePath → reviewed bundle).
- **Terms (draft, founder discretion):** discounted/waived setup in exchange for: (a) candid feedback, (b) a
  named reference **only if they genuinely have a good experience** (never fabricate or pre-promise a quote),
  (c) a case-study conversation. Keep recurring closer to standard so you're validating willingness to pay.
- **Duration:** 2–3 weeks to a delivered bundle; convert to a paid recurring plan at the end.
- **Capacity guardrail:** cap concurrent onboardings at what Brian can review well (~3–5/week). Waitlist the rest.

## Pilot → paid conversion
At bundle delivery: "Here's your package + checksum. To keep this current for your annual affirmation —
re-score after changes, 180-day POA&M tracking, affirmation prep — that's the [Managed/Complete] plan at
[price]. Want me to turn it on?" The retention hook is the affirmation; a one-time SSP churns 25–40%/yr.
