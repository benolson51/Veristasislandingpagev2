## The clock and the math (why this works)

- **Phase 2 (C3PAO stage) starts Mon Nov 10 — 18 weeks away.** That's the selling season. Honest urgency,
  not manufactured.
- Of 80 rows: **75 workable** (5 excluded/deprioritized). ~39 have named signers today; ~20 need an
  owner-ID or gate resolved; ~10 are non-CA (mail wave).
- **Cadence: 2 new touches per workday = 10/week.** All 75 get touch #1 by early September (8 weeks),
  leaving Sept–Oct for follow-ups, diagnostics, and closes. Time cost: ~45 min/day.
- **Funnel (playbook assumptions, replace with real rates on Fridays):** 75 touched × ~10% reply ≈ 8
  cold conversations + the 5 warm/channel = **~12 conversations → 6–8 diagnostics → 3–5 quotes →
  1–3 closed** by Nov 10. Brian's review capacity (3–5/wk) is never the constraint at this volume.
- **Goals:** first paid deal by **Aug 15** · 3 customers by **Oct 1** · 6–8 by **Nov 10** (capacity-bound).

## What can kill this plan (pre-mortem)

1. **The loop not running.** 80 rows at 0 touches/wk = 0 deals. The plan is the rhythm, not the list.
2. **Verifying emails one-at-a-time mid-week** — that's how send-days die. Sunday batch, always.
3. **Deliverability:** never send from a guessed address; warm inbox = max ~15/day from ben@; no images
   in touch #1; plain text beats pretty.
4. **A hot reply rotting.** Reply SLA: same business day, even if just "grabbing us 2 times."
5. **Overbooking Brian.** A missed delivery date costs more than a lost prospect. Waitlist without shame.
