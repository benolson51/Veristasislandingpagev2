# 06 — Launch & Content

_A launch the two of you can actually run, plus a lead magnet that earns trust. All copy §12-clean._

## The launch moment (ship in Week 4 — ~Jul 7–13)
Not a product-hunt spectacle — a credible, founder-voice announcement to the DIB that VeriStasis is open for
business, paired with a genuinely useful free resource. Sequence:
1. **Pre-launch teaser (end of Week 1, ~Jun 19–22):** Ben posts a short "we're building this and
   why" note + a link to join the waitlist. Warms the audience; starts collecting emails.
2. **Launch posts (Week 4):** Ben publishes the announcement (below); Brian optionally reshares; DM warm
   contacts the link individually (don't mass-tag).
3. **Lead magnet live (by launch):** the free CMMC L2 readiness checklist gated behind the waitlist form.
4. **Follow-the-launch:** every positive comment/DM → a calendar link. Feed replies into the pipeline (08).

## Founder LinkedIn announcement (template — Ben's voice; Brian optional reshare)
```
For two years the small defense shops I talk to have said the same thing: a prime sent a CMMC clause with a
deadline, the consultant quote was ~$40k and two months, and nobody on staff owns compliance.

So we built VeriStasis: you answer a scoped intake, and it generates your System Security Plan, POA&M, and
policies, computes your real SPRS score, and shows the shortest legitimate path to raise it — then packages
it into a C3PAO-ready bundle with a checksum your assessor can verify. No AI black box; it's deterministic
and explainable. A CMMC expert reviews it before you certify.

Phase 2 — when Level 2 work needs a C3PAO certificate — starts Nov 10, 2026, and assessor slots are scarce.
If CMMC L2 is bearing down on you, here's a free readiness checklist (no sales call required): [link]

We're onboarding a small number of contractors now. If that's you, DM me.
```
**"Why we built this" long-form (optional, one founder, week of launch):** same story, expanded — the
problem, the FCA-liability reality the signer carries, why determinism/honesty matter to that buyer, and what
VeriStasis deliberately is *not*. End with the checklist + waitlist link.

## Where to post / be present
- **LinkedIn** (primary): founders' own feeds; relevant **CMMC / NIST 800-171 / DIB** groups; comment
  usefully on prime/CMMC threads (don't spam-link).
- **Communities:** Reddit r/NISTControls and r/CMMC (contribute answers, link only when genuinely helpful and
  allowed); CMMC-focused Slack/Discord/forums.
- **Cyber AB Marketplace:** list VeriStasis if/when eligible (also surfaces channel partners — see 01).
- **APEX Accelerators / MEP / NDIA chapters:** offer a free "CMMC L2 readiness" lunch-and-learn or webinar;
  these orgs are hungry for that content and put you in front of clusters of ICP.

## Lead magnet (build once, use everywhere)
Pick ONE, genuinely useful, honesty-first:
- **"CMMC Level 2 Readiness Checklist"** — the 110-control families, what each requires in plain English,
  the must-meet **gating** controls you can't POA&M, and how SPRS scoring deducts points. A real tool, not a
  brochure. Naturally demonstrates the depth behind VeriStasis.
- _Alt:_ **"What your SPRS score actually means"** — how the −203→110 methodology works and what a passing
  number takes. Same spirit.
Gate it behind the waitlist email form. Deliver instantly. Add a soft "want us to just generate this for
you? [calendar link]" at the end. **No fabricated stats; cite the rule where you reference dates/scoring.**

## Waitlist mechanism (cheap + fast)
- The existing site's contact form can double as the waitlist once its endpoint is wired (Formspree /
  Web3Forms — see the site README). Add a dedicated `/#waitlist` anchor or a simple "Join the waitlist"
  variant that just needs name + work email + "what's your CMMC trigger?".
- Pipe submissions to the CRM and a shared inbox; reply personally within one business day.

## Content cadence (sustainable for two busy founders)
- **2 LinkedIn posts/week per founder** during the 30 days (one insight, one proof/behind-the-scenes).
- **1 longer artifact** total in the month (the lead magnet or the "why we built this" post).
- Repurpose: every objection in [02](02-positioning-and-messaging.md) is a post; every ScorePath/bundle
  screenshot (with fake/sample data clearly labeled) is a post. Don't manufacture a content machine you can't
  sustain mid-launch — outbound is the priority; content compounds in the background.
