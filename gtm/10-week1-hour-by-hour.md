# 10 — Ben's 5-Day Go-To-Market Sprint (hour by hour)

_Your solo sell-it sprint, Ben. This is the zoomed-in Week 1 of [07-30-day-plan.md](07-30-day-plan.md).
You own everything that touches a prospect; **Brian owns the technical setup** and runs in parallel so you're
never blocked._

## Roles
- **Ben (you) = all go-to-market:** targeting, messaging, list-building, outreach, demos, launch, RPO
  conversations.
- **Brian = all technical:** the separate sending domain + DNS/deliverability, the demo environment, and the
  intake → document generation → expert review when a deal onboards. He's your "phone-a-friend" on any deep
  technical/security question in a demo.

## What Brian must finish this week (your dependencies — chase him)
- **By end of Day 2:** sending domain live + Google Workspace inboxes + SPF/DKIM/DMARC + **warmup started**
  (so your email lands), and the **waitlist form** wired on the site.
- **By Day 3:** a clean **demo environment** (sample org + data) you can screen-share.
- **Standing:** on-call to join demos for deep technical Qs, and to run intake/expert-review at onboarding.

## Assumptions
Working window **8:00 AM–6:00 PM**, ~1 hr lunch/buffer; not every hour filled. Sprint days: **Tue Jun 16,
Wed Jun 17, Thu Jun 18, Fri Jun 19 (Juneteenth — light), Mon Jun 22.** Weekend Jun 20–21 optional. "Done ="
is the concrete output that proves the hour worked.
