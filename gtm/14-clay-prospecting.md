# 14 — Clay Prospecting: finish + recreate

_Your Find People search is configured (2,187 targeted leads). These are the last clicks to build the table
and get emails — do them in a normal Clay tab (if it glitches/zooms, reload once)._

## What's already set up
A **Find People** search targeting the ICP: Owners / CEOs / Presidents / Founders / COOs at U.S. defense,
aerospace/aviation, machine-shop, precision-manufacturing, and engineering firms (11–200 employees), CUI /
CMMC Level 2, prioritizing DoD-connected companies. **2,187 results.** Confirmed real fits: Jeh Aerospace,
MRS Machining, Nemean Solutions, Swift Engineering, L2 Aviation, etc.

Find it anytime: **Find leads → People → "See past searches."**

## Finish it (4 clicks — spends credits, so start small)
1. **Cap the batch first** (protects your free credits): in the left filter list, open **"Limit results"** and
   set **100**. (Skipping this imports up to 1,000 = likely your whole free balance.)
2. Click blue **Continue → "Save to new workbook and table."** → builds the lead table (name · title · company).
3. In the table: **+ Add column → Enrich → "Find work email"** (a.k.a. work-email / waterfall email). Run it
   on the 100 rows. **This is the main credit cost** — confirm quality on a few before scaling.
4. **Export:** top-right **Export → CSV** (or push to your CRM). Then load into the [outbound playbook](03-outbound-playbook.md).

Once you've got name + title + company + email, that's a working cold list to run behind your warm push.

## Credit math (free plan)
- Importing rows and finding emails both cost credits. Free plans are limited — that's why step 1 caps at 100.
- Validate the first 100 (are the companies on-target? are the emails landing?), then scale toward the full
  2,187 in batches once you trust it (or once you upgrade).
