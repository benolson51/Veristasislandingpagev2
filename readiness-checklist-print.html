The CMMC Level 2 Readiness Checklist

A plain-English reality check for the owner who signs the SPRS affirmation.

Why you, specifically: you're the senior official who signs the affirmation — and that signature carries personal False Claims Act exposure. Ten minutes here is worth it. If you can't confidently check these boxes, you're not assessment-ready yet.

1. Do you actually have the three mandatory artifacts?

  • A System Security Plan addressing all 110 NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 controls — boundary, system description, status of each.
  • A POA&M with a remediation plan, owner, and milestone for every gap.
  • A policy set (one per control family) plus core procedures, control-linked.
  • A SPRS score posted — and you know the number.

2. The SPRS score math (where owners get surprised)

You don't earn points up from zero — you start at 110 and lose 5, 3, or 1 point for every unmet control, down to a −203 floor. So "we're ~70% there" is not a score of 70. If your gaps are the heavy controls, your real posted score can be deeply negative — we routinely see shops shocked their starting number is below zero.
  • You know your actual current SPRS score (not a guess).
  • You know which specific unmet controls are dragging it down most.

3. The gating-control trap (the one that fails assessments)

Not every gap can be deferred. To use a POA&M at all you must already be at ≥ 80% (SPRS ≥ 88) on assessment day. And certain controls are "must-meet" — they cannot go on a POA&M. If one of those is open, you fail outright, regardless of score. POA&M items must close within 180 days. Most shops learn which controls are non-negotiable only when a C3PAO tells them — the expensive way.
  • You know the 80% / SPRS-88 threshold required to even use a POA&M.
  • You know which of your open controls are "must-meet" (non-deferrable).

4. Scope & the things that quietly sink shops

  • You've defined your CUI boundary — what systems/people actually touch CUI (smaller honest scope = fewer controls).
  • You've handled 3.13.11 — FIPS-validated cryptography for CUI (a requirement, and a common gap; "we use AES" isn't automatically enough).
  • You have a Customer Responsibility Matrix + ESP service description for providers in your boundary (cloud, MSP, tooling).
  • Inherited controls (M365 GCC High, Google Workspace, AWS GovCloud) are documented, not assumed.

5. The affirmation & the clock

  • You know who signs (you) and that it recurs annually.
  • You know Phase 2 — when Level 2 work needs a C3PAO certificate — begins November 10, 2026, and assessor capacity is scarce.
  • You've accepted readiness typically takes months, not weeks — so the time to know your gap is now.

Score yourself

0–4 boxes: exposed. A flow-down today would catch you flat.
5–9 boxes: started — but the gating controls and SPRS math are where deals get lost.
10+ boxes: in good shape. Keep it current for the annual affirmation.
