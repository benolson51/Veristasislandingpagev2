Your email-signature logo

Transparent PNG, sized for an email signature — reads cleanly on Gmail's white background. One click → download → insert.

  1. Click Download PNG (saves a transparent veristasis-signature-logo.png).
  2. Gmail → ⚙ → See all settings → General tab → Signature.
  3. Click the 🖼️ insert-image icon → Upload → pick the PNG → place it above your name.
  4. Click the image once, set width to ~180px, then Save Changes (bottom of the page).
